        Quick start

        Five minutes to set up, then one tap per task. The point is not to prove you are slow: it is to show how much of your day is work that does not need a physician or PA.

        1. Bookmark this page on each phone and computer you use. Entries are saved in the browser you log them in, so use the same one each time.
        2. Tap a task when you start it. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows the timer. Buttons with a blue edge are work that needs a physician or PA; highlighted buttons are work support staff could do, and each says who.
        3. While it runs, add detail if you like (all optional): who asked, "Interrupted a visit", and on a support task "Needed a clinician" if this one needed a physician or PA, for example a call where you made a clinical decision.
        4. Tap the task again, or Stop and save, when you finish. Tapping a different task saves this one and starts the next. There is nothing to fill in.
        5. Forgot to tap? Open , then "Add work you forgot to time". Choose the date it actually happened.
        6. Save a backup file every few days. The line under the title says what is not backed up yet, and turns amber when it is time.
        7. At the end of the week, open , choose the dates, and use Copy summary to paste the figures into an email to your supervisor.

        No patient identifiers. Never type a patient's name, MRN, date of birth or anything else that identifies them. Generic labels only, such as "DME order follow-up, wheelchair".

        Frequently asked questions

        Tracking

        What should I time?

        All of it: visits, inbox, notes, calls, forms, chasing, coordinating. The work that is not patient-facing is the work nobody sees in a schedule, so it matters most. Short tasks count too; each saved entry is at least 1 minute.

        A task I do has no button. What do I use?

        Add one on the tab: give it a name, the clinic, and who could do it instead. Or use the closest button and add a short note to the entry. A growing pile of work that does not fit is itself evidence that the job is bigger than its description. You can also suggest a button with the feedback form.

        I tapped the wrong button, or a preset is wrong for this one.

        Open and use Edit on the entry. You can change the task, minutes, date, clinic, who could do it, source, note and the ticks (only the start time cannot be changed). Delete entry is there too.

        A call turned into a clinical decision. Is it still support work?

        Not if it needed you. Tick "Needed a clinician" in the timer bar (or change "Who could do it" on the entry afterwards) and it counts as physician or PA work. Family calls and results follow-up default to RN because triage and relaying results that are already classified are generally RN work; the tick covers the ones that were not.

        I left a timer running.

        When a timer has run longer than 2 h, the bar turns brown and Stop asks how long the work actually took. Type minutes (45) or hours and minutes (1:30), discard it, or keep timing.

        What if I close the page, or my phone locks, while timing?

        The timer keeps going. When you open the page again it is still running from when you started it.

        What counts as after hours?

        Work that starts before 8am or from 5pm, or on a weekend. It is set from the start time; change it on the entry if it is wrong.

        What does "patient-facing" mean?

        A patient or family was directly involved at the time, such as a visit or a call with a parent. Forms, chasing, documenting and coordinating are not patient-facing: that is the invisible work.

        What are the healthy urgent buttons for?

        Work for non-complex, healthy urgent patients that has been assigned to you on top of your complex care clinic. The report counts it separately, so it shows how much of your time goes to work outside your own clinic.

        Can I have the tracker open in two tabs or windows?

        Yes. A change saved in one shows up in the other.

        The report

        What does the headline percentage mean?

        The share of the time you logged in the chosen dates that did not need a physician or PA: work that support staff could have done ().

        What is the support-FTE estimate, and why is it missing?

        Support hours a week divided by 40: how much of a full-time support job you are doing yourself. For example, 12 hours a week is about 0.3 of a support FTE. It needs a date range of at least 7 days, so it is missing on "Today", and on "This week" until Sunday: choose Last 7 days or Last 4 weeks. Days before your first entry are not counted, so it also needs a full week of logging.

        How do I share it with my supervisor?

        On , choose the dates and use Copy summary: it is plain text for an email. Export CSV gives every entry in those dates for a spreadsheet. Two to four ordinary weeks make a stronger case than one.

        Will this make it look as if I am slow?

        The report is about volume and who the work belongs to, not speed. It shows hours of work that did not need a physician or PA, and so how much support the clinic needs to return that time to patients.

        Backups, phones and computers

        Where are my entries saved?

        Only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere: the page makes no network requests at all. That also means clearing browsing data, a private window, or a work computer that resets when you sign out can erase them. A backup file protects them.

        How do I back up?

        Open (it is also under the title) and use Save backup file. When a backup is due, a Save backup file button also appears under the title. On a computer the file goes to Downloads; on a phone the share sheet opens so you can email it to yourself or save it to Files or a cloud drive. Keep it somewhere other than the device.

        How do I use it on my phone and my computer?

        Each browser keeps its own entries. To move them, save a backup file on one and use Restore from backup file on the other. New entries are added, and an entry already there is replaced only by a more recently edited copy, so restoring the same file twice does no harm. Deleted entries stay deleted, and deletions travel in backups too, unless the entry was edited on the other device after it was deleted: then the edit wins.

        I use an iPhone.

        Safari can clear a site's saved data if it is not opened for about a week, so save backups regularly. Adding the page to the Home Screen avoids that, but the Home Screen icon keeps its own entries, separate from Safari: save a backup in one and restore it in the other to switch.

        A backup was refused as "from a newer version".

        The page you are using is an older copy, such as a downloaded file. Open the tracker at its usual link, which is always the latest version, and restore the file there.

        Changing the tracker

        Can I rename, add or remove buttons?

        Yes, on the tab. Changes apply to new taps; saved entries keep what they had, so a renamed button's time counts from its next tap. Button changes are saved in this browser and travel in backup files.

        Can I change clinic hours or role names?

        Yes, in the settings block at the top of tracker.html (between SETTINGS START and SETTINGS END). If an edit there would make the report wrong, a yellow "Check the settings" notice says what to fix.

        Tracking as a team

        How do several clinicians make one report?

        Agree on the same 2 to 4 ordinary weeks. Everyone tracks on their own device, then uses Save backup file and sends the file to whoever builds the report. That person opens and uses Add backup files. People are shown as Clinician A, Clinician B, and so on, never by name.

        Are the team's files stored in my browser?

        No. They are only read for the report, never added to your own entries, and forgotten when the page is closed or reloaded.
